The house

A century-old merchant's home, restored by hand

Behind an unmarked cedar door in the Derb Sidi Bouloukat, Dar Lumière was built in 1924 for a family of spice traders. Over three years, master craftsmen from Fès restored its tadelakt walls, carved plaster and painted ceilings — then we added the things a modern traveller wants: silent air-conditioning, rain showers, fast Wi-Fi and the best mint tea in the medina.

We are small on purpose. With nine rooms, our team knows every guest by name, and every breakfast is cooked to order.
